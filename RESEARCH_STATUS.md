# Research Status

**Verified Reciprocal Evolution**  
**Author:** James Paul Jackson  
**Publication review date:** 16 November 2026, Eastern time

Verified Reciprocal Evolution is a framework and preliminary-results white paper. It proposes an architecture and a testing discipline for persistent non-LLM learning, recursive policy adaptation, and receiver-owned transfer. The publication does not claim general intelligence, universal transfer, independently established superiority, or academic peer-review acceptance.

## Demonstrated mechanisms and measured limits

| Evidence | Current interpretation |
|:--|:--|
| Agent Colony's specialist population, communication bus, Oracle, Hive/Bitwork routing, self-change trials, and twin control | Implemented computational-swarm architecture. Cumulative activity is not evidence of a causal learning or communication advantage. |
| Agent Colony's preregistered holdout | Learning-on improved within-run selection coverage, but harmful keeps increased and the safety-constrained success criterion failed. |
| Agent Colony's cross-version comparison | No measurable gain on the frozen holdout: the compared versions produced identical results on the paired seeds. |
| One Mind's changed evolutionary policies | An experimental intervention fork. Its effects require a clean baseline and independent evaluation. Inherited code and counters are not independent replications. |
| SYMBIOTE's restarted fresh-input predictions | Bounded persistent conditional structural learning beyond an exact answer cache. The isolated structural learner tied. |
| SYMBIOTE's rank-based query policy | Reduced declared work in a scoped inference operation. It did not reduce source query count or establish whole-system economic superiority. |
| SYMBIOTE's separate receiver-outcome trace | Receiver-owned evaluation, adoption, and retained-case use within a bounded proof envelope. This is distinct from fresh-task peer transfer or bilateral economic benefit. |

## Historical evidence boundaries

Reported values are tied to the manuscript's cited revisions. The relevant Agent Colony record is dated 17 November UTC, corresponding to 16 November Eastern. Live repository state may differ.

The [Agent Colony holdout report](https://github.com/jacksonjp0311-gif/agent-colony/blob/4304c25d78dab105f78cfbb9c97bf665e793bbf5/evidence/2026-10-16-holdout-v2/README.md) records means of **4.0** verified cases per seed with learning and **1.6** without, alongside **0.4** versus **0.2** harmful keeps. These are five paired seeds over a restricted candidate pool, not independent evidence of broad generalization. The failed criterion and absence of cross-version improvement remain part of the result.

The [SYMBIOTE engineering report](https://github.com/jacksonjp0311-gif/SYMBIOTE/blob/482bf104c89721965ec006cc9f5b9f37237278bd/docs/ENGINE_EVOLUTION_002.md) and [machine-readable summary](https://github.com/jacksonjp0311-gif/SYMBIOTE/blob/482bf104c89721965ec006cc9f5b9f37237278bd/experiments/engine-evolution-002/summary.json) record four width-eight cases, each trained with nine observations and one separate validation observation. Restarted structural arms correctly predicted 32 previously unanswered inputs per case, totaling 128 case/input pairs per arm. The isolated structural learner tied. These cases belong to a supported affine family; they do not establish unfamiliar-domain transfer.

Reference and rank-based inference used **28,439** and **713** declared GF(2) work units per case, respectively, with the same ten source queries. Operation counts, storage, source computation, exchange, verification, and supervision costs must remain distinguishable.

The separate receiver-feedback trace recorded evaluated adoption and use of a retained case. Strict verified economic benefit remained zero because costs were unknown. The historical [primary-study HOLD](https://github.com/jacksonjp0311-gif/SYMBIOTE/blob/482bf104c89721965ec006cc9f5b9f37237278bd/docs/RESEARCH_V05_PRIMARY_HOLD.md) and [NOT_DEMONSTRATED transfer protocol](https://github.com/jacksonjp0311-gif/SYMBIOTE/blob/482bf104c89721965ec006cc9f5b9f37237278bd/docs/RESEARCH_V06_PROTOCOL.md) are not superseded by successful component checks.

## Open hypotheses

The following remain unproven:

- A causal benefit from specialist communication over matched noncommunicating controls.
- Reliable fresh-task gains from recursive policy adaptation without increased harmful acceptance.
- Positive net benefit to both independent receivers after the full costs of exchange and verification.
- Autonomous construction and useful transfer of executable methods to new task families.
- Broad generalization, general intelligence, or universal efficiency superiority.

Successful verification, additional evolution cycles, greater search depth, and retained state are insufficient by themselves to establish these claims. The paper's equations are proposed engineering definitions and evaluation quantities, not established convergence or superiority theorems.

## Next evidence required

The [prospective protocols](reproducibility/PROTOCOL.md) specify matched ablations for retained experience, communication, reciprocal exchange, and method construction. Confirmatory work must freeze code, tasks, evaluators, budgets, metrics, and exclusions before outcomes are exposed. Both directional transfer effects, false acceptances, incomplete runs, and negative results must be reported.

The existing Agent Colony holdout registration retains its original exposure rules. A rerun must be identified as a re-exposure; new confirmatory claims require appropriately independent tasks and registrations.
